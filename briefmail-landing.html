01 — the problem

Three things are quietly broken about how you use email today.

Context fragmentation

The important thing is buried

A ₹15,000 EMI due Friday sits under fourteen unread newsletters. Interview confirmations get lost between promo codes. Nothing about Gmail tells you what matters today.

Manual re-entry

You're the integration layer

Copying expenses into a budget sheet. Pasting job links into Notion. Re-typing meeting URLs into your calendar. Your inbox already has this data — nobody's using it.

Clutter as insurance

You keep everything, just in case

Thousands of promotional emails stay unarchived because one might contain an invoice you'll need in April. Search shouldn't require hoarding.

02 — how it works

A cascade, not a single model.

Every email is expensive to run through an LLM and slow to wait on. BriefMail only escalates when it has to — most mail never reaches Tier 3 at all.

TIER 1

Deterministic rules

Known bank domains, OTP subject patterns, recruitment portals like LinkedIn and Naukri — matched by regex before any model is even loaded. If the rule fires, that's the answer.

<5ms resolves ~70% of mail
TIER 2

Lightweight classifier

Emails Tier 1 can't confidently place get a fast zero-shot pass against a fixed category ontology — still no full LLM call, still fast enough to feel instant.

~200ms handles the ambiguous middle
TIER 3

Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite

Only genuinely unclear mail reaches an LLM — and even then it returns structured JSON: summary, amounts, due dates, meeting links. Nothing is left as an unstructured guess.

~1s last resort, not first pass

Built on a few strict rules, not vibes.

Most inbox tools bolt AI onto search. BriefMail was designed backwards from a simple standard: never guess when a rule will do, and never keep more of your data than the feature needs.

Rules before models

If a deterministic pattern can classify an email with full precision, BriefMail never spends a model call on it.

Privacy by default

Structured metadata and summaries live in Postgres with row-level security — not permanent copies of your full email bodies.

Speed as a feature

Heuristic triage runs in single-digit milliseconds, so the inbox never feels like it's "thinking" before showing you mail.
